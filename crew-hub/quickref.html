Quick reference

Ice protection, starter limits and fuel for the G100, King Air 350 and Challenger 604.

Icing conditions defined

G100

Ground
OAT ≤10°C + moisture OR contaminated
Flight
TAT ≤10°C (SAT ≤5°C if TAT inop) + moisture

KA350

Ground
OAT ≤5°C + moisture cannot be assured
Flight
OAT ≤5°C + visible moisture

CL604

Ground
OAT ≤5°C + (moisture <400' OR wet runway OR precip)†
Flight
TAT ≤10°C + moisture (except SAT ≤–40°C)

Moisture: clouds, fog ≤1 sm, rain, snow, sleet, ice crystals.

Engine / cowl anti-ice

G100

Activate
Ground: OAT ≤10°C + moisture/contaminated
Flight: icing conditions or ice detected
Critical
! Continuous ign BEFORE activation
NAC light ON when able

KA350

Activate
ON: OAT ≤5°C (moisture can't be assured)
OFF: T/O if OAT >10°C
Critical
Lip boot heat AIRBORNE ONLY

CL604

Activate
Ground: OAT ≤10°C + moisture/contaminated†
Flight ≥FL220: ice detect OR icing (detector failed)
Flight <FL220: icing OR ice detected
Critical
! If wing ON for T/O, cowl MUST be ON

Wing anti-ice / de-ice

G100

Type
Continuous anti-ice
Activate
ON when icing conditions exist or are anticipated
Leave ON until clear
Note
Proactive system

KA350

Type
Reactive de-ice
Activate
When ice ½–1 inch (temps >–40°C)
Restrictions
Min 140 KIAS
Min temp –40°C
NO sustained use with flaps

CL604

Type
Proactive anti-ice
Activate
T/O (OAT ≤5°C): ON on final taxi
Flight: ice detect or icing (altitude-dependent)
Restrictions
EXIT NOW if side window ice (SLD)
NO single-engine taxi
Wing ON → cowl ON

Takeoff icing reminders

G100

Remember
Icing → BOTH ON (wing + cowl)
Continuous ign BEFORE cowl

KA350

Remember
OAT ≤5°C → engine anti-ice ON
OAT >10°C → OFF
Boots are reactive (NOT for T/O)

CL604

Remember
OAT ≤5°C: wing ON (prior to thrust if fluid)
Cowl ON
NO single-engine taxi
Verify lights

Quick decision

G100

Ground
OAT ≤10°C + moisture or contaminated → cowl + wing ON
Flight
TAT ≤10°C + moisture → cowl + wing ON
(continuous ign first)

KA350

Ground
OAT ≤5°C + moisture → engine anti-ice ON
Flight
OAT ≤5°C + visible moisture → engine ON
Boots at ½–1 in ice (≥140 KIAS)

CL604

Ground
OAT ≤5°C + moisture → wing + cowl ON for T/O†
Flight
TAT ≤10°C + moisture → wing + cowl ON

† CL604 ground threshold reads ≤5°C in some boxes and ≤10°C in the engine / cowl box. Confirm in the AFM.

Engine starter limits

G100

Cranking
Light-off: 10 sec max
To idle: 60 sec max
Air-start: 45 sec · Windmill: no limit
ITT
Ground ≤120°C
Air ≤90°C

KA350

Cranking
1st: 30 sec ON / 5 min OFF
2nd: 30 sec ON / 5 min OFF
3rd: 30 sec ON / 30 min OFF
Note
The 2nd cycle is normally used to clear residual fuel.

CL604

Cranking
Three cycles · 5 min cooling
Dry 1st: 90 sec / 5 min · 2nd+: 30 sec / 5 min
ITT / N2
Ground ≤120°C
Air ≤90°C
N2 <55%
